:wave: Hi, I'm **Josselin Lefèvre**, a research scientist in image analysis, algorithms and applied AI.

:mortar_board: I hold a PhD in Computer Science, carried out with Thermo Fisher Scientific and LIGM (Université Gustave Eiffel), on segmenting **billion-voxel microscopy images with far less memory**. [Read the story](/blog/2026/scalable-hierarchical-segmentation/).

:microscope: These days I explore how modern AI can work in practice for electron microscopy and scientific imaging: limited data, real-world variability and tools scientists can actually use.
